# The Encentive — Internal Campaign Playbook

An internal marketing campaign for Encountive team members, built around the
company mission and the value we put into the world — powered by the wordplay
hiding inside our own name.

> **Status:** Draft for review. The mission language in §3 is a *proposed*
> articulation (no formal mission statement exists in this repo yet) — get
> leadership sign-off on that section before launch. Everything else is
> ready-to-use copy.

---

## 1. The big idea

Three words live inside our name:

| Hidden word | Where it hides | What it stands for |
|---|---|---|
| **Encounter** | ENCOUNT‑ive | The moments we create — every touchpoint between a product and a person. |
| **Inventive** | Enco‑UNTIVE (anagram energy) | How we work — we build, we automate, we ship creatively. |
| **Incentive** | E‑NCNTIVE → **Encentive** | *Why* we work — the value we put into the world, and what it gives back. |

The campaign coins one word from that last one:

> **Encentive** *(n.)* — the reason behind the work. The value Encountive
> creates for the world, and the pride the world hands back to us.
> *Pronounced exactly like it looks. Spelled like it means it.*

Every campaign asset answers one question in the team's voice:
**"What's your Encentive?"**

And one more letter matters:

> **There's U in EncoUntive.** Right in the middle. On purpose.

That's the people pillar — the mission doesn't move without the team.

---

## 2. Campaign objectives

1. **Internalize the mission** — every team member can say what Encountive
   does for the world in one sentence, their own words.
2. **Connect daily work to value** — engineers, designers, and operators can
   name the "encentive" behind their current project.
3. **Build ritual** — a lightweight peer-recognition habit ("giving an
   Encentive") that outlives the campaign.

Success looks like: campaign vocabulary showing up unprompted in Slack,
stand-ups, and demos.

### Pillar 3 — ENCENTIVE: *We know why.*

- "There's U in EncoUntive."

## 5. The Encentive recognition ritual

The mechanic that makes the campaign stick:

- **Giving an Encentive** = a public peer shout-out that names the *value
  delivered*, not just the effort. Format: `🏅 Encentive to @name — [what
  they did] → [who it helped / what it unlocked]`.
- Posted in a dedicated **#encentives** Slack channel (or the main team
  channel with the 🏅 emoji as the marker).
- **Monthly Encentive Award** — the team votes one shout-out as the
  "Encentive of the Month" at all-hands. Winner picks next month's campaign
  poster line.
- Rule of the ritual: an Encentive always ends with *who it helped*. That's
  the mission muscle being trained.

| 2 | **Reveal** — launch email + all-hands moment: the Encentive definition card | Launch email (§7), definition poster |

| 6 | **Ritual lock-in** — first Monthly Encentive Award; recap carousel of the best moments | Award + recap carousel |

**Dogfooding note:** every campaign carousel should be produced *in Content
Studio itself*. The campaign about our value is rendered by our value.
